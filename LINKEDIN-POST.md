🚀 I’ve built and published my Scrum Master Case Studies & Agile Problem-Solving Portfolio on GitHub.

As I continue growing my Scrum Master journey, I wanted to create something more practical than a collection of Scrum definitions.

So I built a portfolio around realistic Agile delivery situations and explored how a Scrum Master can approach them through:

🎯 Sprint Goal focus  
🤝 Facilitation & coaching  
🔍 Root-cause analysis  
🚧 Impediment & dependency management  
📈 Agile metrics  
🧪 Quality and Definition of Done  
🌱 Continuous improvement  
🏢 Cross-team collaboration & scaling  
🤖 AI-enabled Agile practices

The repository currently includes 10 case studies covering scenarios such as:

• Repeatedly missed Sprint Goals  
• Product Owner priority changes during a Sprint  
• High velocity with declining quality  
• Resistance to Retrospectives  
• Stakeholders assigning work directly to Developers  
• Production emergencies during a Sprint  
• Developer–QA conflict  
• Cross-team dependencies  
• Building a new Scrum Team  
• Scaling Agile across multiple teams

For each case study, I used a practical structure:

Situation → Observation → Root Cause → Scrum Master Intervention → Metrics → Improvement Experiment → Expected Outcome → Learning

I’m especially interested in the intersection of **Scrum, Quality Engineering, continuous improvement, and AI-enabled delivery**.

This portfolio is also intended as a learning resource for aspiring Scrum Masters preparing for interviews and looking to develop practical problem-solving skills.
