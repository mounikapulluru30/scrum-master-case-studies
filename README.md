# Scrum Master Case Studies & Agile Problem-Solving Portfolio

> A practical portfolio demonstrating Scrum Master thinking, Agile problem-solving, facilitation, metrics, continuous improvement, quality, and AI-enabled Agile practices.

## 👋 About This Portfolio

This repository is a learning and portfolio project created to demonstrate how a Scrum Master can approach realistic delivery challenges using Scrum principles, evidence-based problem solving, coaching, facilitation, and continuous improvement.

**Note:** The case studies are scenario-based simulations and learning exercises. They are not presented as claims of specific production incidents or employer experience.

## 🎯 What This Portfolio Demonstrates

- Scrum Master facilitation and servant leadership
- Sprint Goal and outcome focus
- Root-cause analysis and impediment removal
- Product Owner and stakeholder collaboration
- Conflict resolution and team coaching
- Dependency and risk management
- Quality and Definition of Done practices
- Agile metrics and evidence-based improvement
- Retrospective facilitation and action tracking
- Cross-team collaboration and scaling considerations
- Responsible use of AI to support Agile ways of working

## 🧭 Case Study Framework

Each scenario follows:

**Situation → Observation → Root Cause → Scrum Master Intervention → Metrics → Improvement Experiment → Expected Outcome → Learning**

The purpose is to demonstrate structured thinking, coaching mindset, experimentation, inspection, and adaptation—not to prescribe one universally correct response.

## 📊 Agile Metrics

The [`metrics`](metrics/) folder contains examples covering:

- Sprint Goal achievement
- Velocity
- Carryover
- Cycle time
- Blocked time
- Work in Progress (WIP)
- Escaped defects
- Quality trends

> Metrics should support transparency, inspection, adaptation, and system improvement—not rank or evaluate individual team members.

## 🤖 AI-Enabled Scrum Master Practices

AI can responsibly support Scrum Master activities such as:

- Summarising retrospective themes
- Identifying recurring patterns in impediment/action logs
- Drafting facilitation questions
- Analysing Agile metrics
- Generating improvement experiment ideas
- Drafting stakeholder communications
- Supporting backlog refinement preparation

AI should augment human judgement, coaching, collaboration, and accountability—not replace them.

## 💼 Using This Portfolio in Interviews

These case studies demonstrate **how I approach Agile problems** and can support scenario-based interview discussions.

A useful structure is:

1. Clarify the situation and desired outcome.
2. Inspect the available evidence.
3. Identify likely system/root causes.
4. Facilitate the people closest to the problem.
5. Agree on a small improvement experiment.
6. Measure the impact.
7. Inspect and adapt.
